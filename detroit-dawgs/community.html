Community

Fort Myers Beach gave Scott & Trina a second home. This is how Detroit Dawgs gives back.

🎆 Friday Fireworks Fundraiser

Every Friday, Detroit Dawgs donates a portion of sales to local businesses raising funds for the Fort Myers Beach Fourth of July fireworks show. Come hungry on a Friday and you're chipping in too.

🧿 Island Charm Crawl

Detroit Dawgs is a proud stop on the Island Charm Crawl — a summer event where, for $30, the public gets a gift bag and crawls to local businesses collecting charms. It's a fun way to explore the beach while supporting the small businesses that make it special.

🚗 Car Show Events

You'll also find Detroit Dawgs showing up at local car show events on the beach — because nothing pairs better with classic cars than a classic Coney dog.
