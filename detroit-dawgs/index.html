Our Story

A retired Detroit couple, chasing a dream on the beach.

Scott and Trina Legas spent years running a concession business back in Detroit. After retiring and falling in love with Fort Myers Beach as longtime snowbirds, they decided to bring a taste of home south with them.

"We started out for fun," Trina says. "We weren't sure we were going to do it full-time because it's a lot of work." Fun turned into a full-blown second act — and now Detroit Dawgs is a fixture on Estero Blvd.
