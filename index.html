Window-Serve, Beach-Side, No Fuss

Order at the window, grab a seat at one of our brightly painted picnic tables, and let the ocean breeze do the rest. We keep it simple: real Mexican staples — tacos, burritos, quesadillas, and nachos — made to order, served fast, and built for sandy feet and salty air.

Whether you're stopping by after the beach or making us your dinner spot for the night, no reservation required. Just walk up, order, and dig in.
